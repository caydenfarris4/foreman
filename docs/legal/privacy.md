# Foreman Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 17, 2026

**Last updated:** July 17, 2026

Foreman is a coaching app for working managers, operated by **Farris Industries** ("we," "us," "our"). This policy explains what data Foreman collects, why we collect it, who processes it, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language on purpose. If anything here is unclear, email us at cfarriscoaching@gmail.com.

> **The short version.** Foreman stores your account and the coaching content you write. To generate coaching responses, we send your check-in text to Anthropic's Claude API. We use Stripe for payments, Resend for email, and Supabase, Cloudflare, and Upstash to run the service. We do not show ads, we do not sell your data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers. You can export or delete your data at any time.

## Who we are

Foreman is operated by Farris Industries. For any privacy question, data request, or account-deletion request, contact cfarriscoaching@gmail.com.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why we collect it | Who processes it |
|---|---|---|
| Email and password | To create and secure your account and sign you in. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them in plain text. | Supabase (auth & database) |
| Profile and onboarding answers | To personalize your coaching to your role and situation. | Supabase |
| Coaching content: daily check-ins, situations, notes, retros | Core of the app. Stored so you can review your history, and sent to our AI provider to generate your coaching responses. | Supabase (storage); Anthropic (AI responses) |
| Subscription status and purchase history | To manage your subscription and paid-feature access. | Stripe (payments); Supabase (status) |
| Email delivery data (address, send/open status) | To send daily prompts and retro-day emails you signed up for. | Resend |
| IP address and request metadata | To protect the service from abuse via rate limiting, and to serve the app reliably. | Upstash (rate limiting); Cloudflare (hosting) |

## Your coaching content is sensitive, and we treat it that way

People use Foreman to work through real situations at work, including conflicts with coworkers and managers. We know that. Your check-ins, situations, notes, and retros are private to your account. The only people on our side who can access them are you and, where strictly necessary, our administrators for account support, to fix a technical problem, or if we are legally required to. We do not browse your content, and we do not share it for marketing.

## How Foreman uses AI (please read this part)

Foreman's coaching responses are generated by Anthropic's Claude API. When you submit a check-in or ask for coaching, **we send the text you wrote to Anthropic to generate your response.** That text may include whatever you chose to write, including details about your workplace and the people in it.

Anthropic states that, by default, it does not use inputs or outputs from its commercial API (which is what Foreman uses) to train its models. Anthropic retains API data for a limited period for operational and safety purposes and then deletes it under its own terms. You can read Anthropic's position at its [Privacy Center](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training).

If you would rather not have specific details leave Foreman, keep them out of your check-in text. The coaching still works when you describe a situation without naming names.

## Payments

Paid subscriptions are handled by Stripe. Stripe collects and processes your card details directly. **We never see or store your full card number.** We keep your subscription status and a record of your purchases so we can manage your access and support you. Stripe's handling of your payment data is governed by [Stripe's privacy policy](https://stripe.com/privacy).

## Emails

Foreman sends coaching emails — the daily prompt, sabbath reflection, weekly retro reminder, and inspection notices — delivered through Resend. You can stop these at any time using the "Stop these emails" link in any of them, or with the "Pause coaching emails" toggle in Settings, and adjust their timing and rhythm in Settings as well. We will still send essential account and transactional messages (for example, password resets and payment receipts) because they are necessary to operate your account.

## Service providers we rely on

- **Supabase** — database and authentication (stores your account and content).
- **Anthropic** — AI provider that generates coaching responses.
- **Stripe** — payment processing.
- **Resend** — email delivery.
- **Cloudflare** — hosting and delivery.
- **Upstash** — rate limiting to protect the service.

## What we do not do

- We do not show advertisements in Foreman.
- We do not sell your personal data.
- We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
- We do not share your coaching content for any purpose other than generating your responses and running the service described above.

## Data retention

We keep your account and content for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, we delete your personal data from our systems, except where we are required to keep limited records (for example, transaction records Stripe or tax rules require us to retain). Data held by our processors is deleted according to their own retention schedules after we remove it or instruct them to.

## Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your Foreman account and its data at any time, directly in the app:

1. Sign in and open **You → Settings**.
2. In the **Danger zone** at the bottom, choose **Delete account**.
3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Deletion is **immediate and permanent**. It erases your profile and onboarding answers, every daily check-in and coaching response, your situation library, notes and journal, weekly retros and monthly syntheses, growth plans and inspections, and any cohort membership. If you have an active subscription, it is canceled immediately; time already paid for is not refunded. There is no undo and no grace period, so export anything you want to keep before deleting.

Instructions are also available on the web at [foreman.coach/delete-account](https://foreman.coach/delete-account). If you cannot sign in, email cfarriscoaching@gmail.com from the address on your account and we will delete it for you within 30 days and confirm when it is done.

As noted under Data retention, our payment processor (Stripe) retains transaction records as required by law; nothing else is kept after deletion.

## Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider, data is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and access to user content on our side is limited to what support and operations require. No online service can promise perfect security, but we work to keep your data safe and to limit who can reach it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, including the European Union and California, you have rights over your personal data. These include the right to access the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, and export it. Foreman already lets you export your situation library from within the app, and you can request a full export or exercise any of these rights by emailing cfarriscoaching@gmail.com. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

## Children

Foreman is built for working managers and is intended for adults. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact cfarriscoaching@gmail.com and we will delete it.
