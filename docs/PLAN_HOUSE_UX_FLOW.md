# Plan → Build-the-House: an intuitive, momentum-driven flow

A research-backed design for how Foreman should guide a user from a 10-year
vision down to today's work — built on *Under Construction*'s principles and on
evidence-based funnel/behavioral-design patterns. The job of the flow: take an
abstract, distant vision and convert it into a **proximal, concrete, in-this-
session win as fast as possible — then keep manufacturing those wins.**

---

## 0. The core problem we're fixing

Today's funnel **breaks the momentum chain**:

```
Sign up → Onboarding (Identity · Challenge · Cadence) → "Open the site" → TODAY
                                                                  Plan/house lives off
                                                                  on a separate tab,
                                                                  gated behind a cold
                                                                  200-character essay.
```

The house — which is both the emotional hook *and* the goal engine — is invisible
until the user does the **heaviest input in the entire app with zero payoff shown
first.** That is the textbook activation failure ("long setup before payoff"), and
it's exactly why a new user lands on a blank blueprint form and bounces.

The book says the opposite is the whole point:

> *"I wanted to come to the job site with a blueprint."*
> *Framing "gives everything that follows somewhere to attach… without a frame you
> are still standing outside in a pile of good intentions."*
> *"building… without a completed blueprint… without the finished house to point to
> as proof."*

So: **the plan comes first, it's made easy, and the house reacts immediately.**

---

## 1. The spine — one funnel, mapped three ways

The house metaphor *is* the goal-decomposition and progress-visualization device.
Use it at every stage.

| Funnel stage | House stage | Book principle | What the user does | Momentum mechanics (sourced) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Attract** | Empty lot + faint blueprint of the finished house | "Come to the job site with a blueprint" | Sees the finished house *before* doing work | Peak-preview (Kahneman peak-end), Fresh-Start framing (Dai/Milkman) |
| **Activate** *(first session "aha")* | Foundation pours | Foundation = identity, the unseen base | One line of vision → **one 2-minute action for today** | Tiny-habit B=MAP (Fogg), proximal sub-goals (Bandura), small wins (Amabile), endowed progress (Nunes & Drèze) |
| **Commit** | Framing rises, walls go up | Framing gives the vision "somewhere to attach" | Vision → 1-yr → month → week → **one daily**, each with an if-then | Foot-in-the-door (Cialdini), progressive disclosure (NN/g), implementation intentions (Gollwitzer, d≈0.65), smart defaults (Johnson & Goldstein) |
| **Build & Retain** | Rooms fill; six-month **inspection** | Patience, Refinement, Culture; **Pressure = the house gets inspected** | Daily boards → weekly "walk the site" → monthly room reveal → 6-mo inspection | Hooked loop (Eyal), goal-gradient + Zeigarnik, humane streaks/loss-aversion (Duolingo), peak-end milestones, fresh-start comebacks |

## 2. Screen-by-screen recommended flow

### A. Onboarding — end it *on the house*, not on "Today"
Keep the current three steps (Identity · Challenge · Cadence) — they're a good,
low-friction funnel. **Add "The Vision" as the climactic 4th step**, but keep it
light:

- One prompt with a sentence-starter: *"In ten years I want to be the kind of
  leader who…"* — accept **one strong sentence to start** (drop the cold 200-char
  wall; let them deepen later on the Plan tab). Vague answers get nudged toward
  specificity with examples (Locke & Latham: vague goals don't motivate).
- The instant they commit the line, **animate the foundation pouring** under their
  vision. That is the aha — *"I just turned my life vision into something I can
  see."* End onboarding here, on the house, with a single placed board.

### B. The first "Build" moment (session-1 win)
From the vision **+ the challenge they picked in onboarding**, AI scaffolds a
starter cascade: suggest *one* 1-year milestone, *one* this-month focus, and **one
2-minute action for today** the user can accept or edit (default effect removes the
blank page). Session 1 ends with a board locking into place + a celebration
(Fogg's "celebrate" step; Amabile's small win; peak-end). Activation event to
instrument: **"vision created + first daily action done in session 1."**

### C. The Plan tab, going forward
- **Empty state (no plan yet) — the immediate fix.** Not a bare form. Show the
  **empty lot + the 5 stages framed as ghosted blueprint**, an endowed-progress
  cue ("Your foundation is ready — you finished setup"), and a single CTA **"Lay
  the foundation"** that opens a guided, **one-question-per-screen** vision capture.
- **With a plan.** The house hero (already partly built) + the 5 stages, but with
  **one obvious next board highlighted** (the next ghosted piece) rather than five
  equally-weighted empty levels. One open loop, not five (avoid Zeigarnik overload).

### D. The daily loop (Today + Materials & Build)
Run a clean **Hooked loop**: *Trigger* — a behavior-timed prompt tied to their
stated cue ("After your morning coffee, lay today's brick"). *Action* — the
under-30-second daily board. *Variable reward* — vary the celebration (a board,
occasionally a "bonus room," a reflective note, a glimpse of how far the house has
come). *Investment* — each entry deepens the house and ties the brick back to its
room → vision ("this brick builds the kitchen → moves you toward your 10-year
vision").

### E. The longer rhythm
- **Weekly retro = "walk the site"** — what got built, how the house grew, set next
  week's if-then. A recurring positive peak-end + re-commitment (default it on).
- **Monthly = a room reveal** — full-screen before/after, shareable image (a
  deliberate peak; recognition is a documented retention lever).
- **Six-month = the Inspection** — the book's climax ("every house gets inspected").
  This is the integrity check, the peak-end of the whole arc — already built as the
  Growth Inspection. Frame it as the payoff the build has been earning.

### F. Comebacks (lapsed users)
Fresh-start framing, **never shame** (matches the book's "no streaks, no shame"):
*"New week — your house is still standing. Lay one brick to pick up where you left
off."* Show the structure **intact** (loss preserved), timed to the user's behavior,
not a calendar blast.

## 3. The momentum mechanics to add (prioritized)

1. **Move vision capture into onboarding's climax + show the foundation pour.**
   Fixes the funnel break; creates the session-1 aha. *(activation, peak-end)*
2. **Endowed progress with a stated reason** — the house starts ~12% built
   *"because you laid the foundation in setup."* The reason is mandatory or the
   effect vanishes. *(Nunes & Drèze)*
3. **Progressive disclosure of the cascade** — reveal one level at a time,
   foot-in-the-door; spotlight the single next step. *(Cialdini, NN/g)*
4. **Implementation intentions on weekly/daily goals** — require "When ___, I will
   ___." Single highest-ROI evidence-based feature for follow-through. *(Gollwitzer,
   d≈0.65)*
5. **AI-scaffolded decomposition** — from vision + challenge, propose
   1-yr/month/week/today to accept or edit. Kills the blank page. *(default effect)*
6. **Variable reward + the build animation on board placement.** *(Hooked)*
7. **Humane streak — "the build holds"** (scaffolding freeze / easy recovery). Loss
   aversion *with* a safety net; matches the book's ethos. *(Duolingo)*
8. **Empty-state house on the Plan tab** — ships now, fixes the bare-form moment.
9. **Peak milestone moments** — room/floor complete = full-screen reveal + share.
10. **Fresh-start comeback flows + behavior-timed (not calendar) prompts.**

---

## 4. Guardrails from the book (keep it moral, not manipulative)

*Under Construction* is explicitly anti-shame, anti-shortcut, written "from the job
site," and the Inspection asks about **quality, not effort.** Encouragingly, the
research's anti-patterns line up with the book's ethos:

- No dark patterns or **punitive streaks** — a single miss must never demolish
  visible progress (that's the resentment failure mode, and it violates the book).
- The Inspection is an **integrity check, not a vanity score.**
- Reward **substance** — boards that map to real growth — not hollow points.

---

## 5. Anti-patterns to avoid (from the research)

- The long-setup wall (full 10-yr→daily tree before any payoff) — #1 churn driver.
- Vague vision capture ("be successful") — force specificity.
- Goals without if-then plans — forfeits a d≈0.65 effect.
- Endowed progress with **no stated reason** — kills the effect.
- Punitive streaks / shame on lapse — always ship a safety net.
- Zeigarnik overload — surface **one** obvious next board, not many.
- Identical, predictable rewards — vary them.
- Calendar nags instead of behavior-timed prompts.
- No auto-save on a multi-session plan — auto-save every step.
- Forgettable session endings — close on a visible win.

---

## 6. Suggested build order

- **Phase 1 (ships now, small):** Plan empty-state house + "Lay the foundation"
  CTA → guided one-question-per-screen vision capture. Removes the bare-form moment.
- **Phase 2:** Vision step inside onboarding + foundation-pour aha + endowed
  progress (house starts >0%, with the stated reason).
- **Phase 3:** AI-scaffolded decomposition + progressive-disclosure cascade +
  implementation intentions on weekly/daily goals.
- **Phase 4:** Daily Hooked-loop polish (variable reward), milestone peak reveals,
  humane streak, fresh-start comeback flows, behavior-timed prompts.
